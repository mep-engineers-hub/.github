# Code of Conduct

This community adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/


## Enforcement contact

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **hussein.ragab@mepautomate.com**. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Community-specific rules

In addition to the Covenant, these are treated as conduct issues and handled by locking, editing or removing content:

1. Posting copyrighted standard content (tables, figures, excerpts, PDFs).
2. Posting client, project or site data.
3. Presenting a tool or result as code-compliant, certified or approved.
4. Promotion of paid products without a clear disclosure.
